# Task 2 官方描述语（现行版，逐条锚点化）

**版本**：IELTS Writing Band Descriptors，2023 年 5 月修订版（现行）。Task 2 四项 × Band 9–5 完整，Band 4–1 摘要。
**来源**：British Council 官方 PDF `ielts_writing_band_descriptors.pdf`（Task 1 与 Task 2 合并本，2023 年 5 月更新）；交叉核对 IDP 官方文章与 ielts.org 公开页。见 `sources.md` 第 1–3 条。
**用法**：每一条英文都必须原样引用，不许改写后再说是"官方描述语"。打分时写 `[R:T2.TR.7]` 这样的引用号，输出里能回查到具体一条。

> **版本陷阱（必须先知道）**：2023 年 5 月之前的旧版写法是 `addresses all parts of the task`；现行版是 `the main parts of the prompt are appropriately addressed`。网上和旧教材里大量流传的是旧版。引用旧版当"官方标准"，会被懂行的人一眼看出。本节全部是现行版。

## 元规则（官方对"怎么用描述语"的规定）

<!-- chunk: META.1 | 元规则 / 描述语的匹配逻辑 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
A script must fully fit the positive features of the descriptor at a particular level. Bolded text indicates negative features that will limit a rating.
（一份答卷必须**完整符合**某一档的全部正面特征，才算这一档。加粗部分是"会拉低评级"的负面特征。）

**这条决定了打分方式**：不是"哪一档看起来最像"，而是"从 9 往下找，第一个能被完整满足的档"。9 分差一条就掉到 8，8 分差一条就掉到 7。

<!-- chunk: META.2 | 元规则 / 四项等权与取整 | src: IELTS-Scoring-In-Detail -->
Each of the four criteria counts for 25% of the marks for that task. The score for each task is the average of the four criteria.
（四项各占该篇 25%，四项平均即为该篇分数。）

Task 2 占写作总分的三分之二，Task 1 占三分之一。写作总分 = Task 1 ÷ 3 + Task 2 × 2 ÷ 3。

<!-- chunk: META.3 | 元规则 / 不足字数的后果 | src: BC-WBD-2023, IELTS-Scoring-In-Detail -->
Responses of 20 words or fewer are rated at Band 1. Any copied rubric must be discounted.
（20 词以内一律 Band 1；抄题目的词不计入字数。）

Task 1 少于 150 词、Task 2 少于 250 词属于 underlength。官方没有"每少 10 词扣 0.5"这种公式，但字数不足会直接削弱 TR/TA 的 "fully extended / sufficiently developed" 证据，实际后果通常是把 TR/TA 压在 6 分以下。

<!-- chunk: META.4 | 元规则 / 背诵与抄袭 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
Should only be used where a candidate did not attend or attempt the question in any way, used a language other than English throughout, or where there is proof that a candidate's answer has been totally memorised.
（Band 0 的三种情形之一是"有证据证明整篇背诵"。）

注意用词是 "totally memorised"（完全背诵）。网上流传的"用了模板句就直接 4 分"没有官方依据，属于**待核**。但背模板确实是负分行为：Band 6 及以下的 LR 描述语里有 "memorised phrases, formulaic language"，是明确列出的扣分特征。

## Task Response（TR，任务回应）

<!-- chunk: T2.TR.9 | Task 2 / Task Response / Band 9 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The prompt is appropriately addressed and explored in depth. A clear and fully developed position is presented which directly answers the question/s. Ideas are relevant, fully extended and well supported. Any lapses in content or support are extremely rare.

（题目每一部分都答到并挖深；立场清楚且完整展开，直接回答题目所问；观点相关、充分延展、有力支撑；内容上的疏漏极少。）
观察点：每个主体段都有一个"机制 + 后果"，且全文找不到一段可以整段删掉而不影响论证。

<!-- chunk: T2.TR.8 | Task 2 / Task Response / Band 8 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The prompt is appropriately and sufficiently addressed. A clear and well-developed position is presented in response to the question/s. Ideas are relevant, well extended and supported. There may be occasional omissions or lapses in content.

（题目答得充分；立场清楚且展开良好；观点相关、延展、有支撑；偶尔有一处内容疏漏。）
观察点：有一处例子偏笼统、或某个限定词没回应到，仍可站 8；出现"整体方向对但某一段没落地"，就是 7。

<!-- chunk: T2.TR.7 | Task 2 / Task Response / Band 7 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The main parts of the prompt are appropriately addressed. A clear and developed position is presented. Main ideas are extended and supported but there may be a tendency to over-generalise or there may be a lack of focus and precision in supporting ideas/material.

（题目的**主要部分**被恰当回应；立场清楚且有展开；主要观点有延展和支撑，但可能偏向泛泛而谈，或支撑材料不够聚焦精准。）
观察点：`many companies` `some countries` `people often` 这类笼统主体出现两次以上，就是 7 而不是 8。这是 2023 版**放松**的地方：不再要求"答全每一个部分"，只要求"主要部分"。

<!-- chunk: T2.TR.6 | Task 2 / Task Response / Band 6 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The main parts of the prompt are addressed (though some may be more fully covered than others). An appropriate format is used. A position is presented that is directly relevant to the prompt, although the conclusions drawn may be unclear, unjustified or repetitive. Main ideas are relevant, but some may be insufficiently developed or may lack clarity, while some supporting arguments and evidence may be less relevant or inadequate.

（主要部分都提到了，但明显有的答得足、有的答得虚；格式合适；立场与题目相关，但结论可能含糊、没根据或重复；主要观点相关，但有些没展开或不清楚，部分论据不贴或不足。）
观察点：有明显"一段很饱满、另一段只有三句"的失衡，就是 6。

<!-- chunk: T2.TR.5 | Task 2 / Task Response / Band 5 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The main parts of the prompt are incompletely addressed. The format may be inappropriate in places. The writer expresses a position, but the development is not always clear. Some main ideas are put forward, but they are limited and are not sufficiently developed and/or there may be irrelevant detail. There may be some repetition.

（题目**没答全**；格式局部不合适；有立场但展开不清；观点少、没铺开，可能夹着离题细节；可能有重复。）
观察点：双问句只答一个、discuss both views 只写一方 —— 都是 5 分的 "incompletely addressed"，不是 6 分。

<!-- chunk: T2.TR.4 | Task 2 / Task Response / Band 4 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The prompt is tackled in a minimal way, or the answer is tangential, possibly due to some misunderstanding of the prompt. The format may be inappropriate. A position is discernible, but the reader has to read carefully to find it. Main ideas are difficult to identify and such ideas that are identifiable may lack relevance, clarity and/or support. Large parts of the response may be repetitive.

（只做了最低限度回应，或答得偏、可能理解错了题；格式可能不合适；立场要读者仔细找；主要观点难辨认，能辨认的也可能不相关、不清楚、没支撑；大量重复。）

## Coherence and Cohesion（CC，连贯与衔接）

<!-- chunk: T2.CC.9 | Task 2 / Coherence and Cohesion / Band 9 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The message can be followed effortlessly. Cohesion is used in such a way that it very rarely attracts attention. Any lapses in coherence or cohesion are minimal. Paragraphing is skilfully managed.

（读起来毫不费力；衔接用得几乎不引人注意；连贯疏漏极少；分段处理得很老练。）
观察点：好衔接是"你看不出他在衔接"。连接词用得越显眼，越不是 9。

<!-- chunk: T2.CC.8 | Task 2 / Coherence and Cohesion / Band 8 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The message can be followed with ease. Information and ideas are logically sequenced, and cohesion is well managed. Occasional lapses in coherence and cohesion may occur. Paragraphing is used sufficiently and appropriately.

（读起来轻松；信息与观点排序合乎逻辑；衔接管理良好；偶有疏漏；分段充分且恰当。）

<!-- chunk: T2.CC.7 | Task 2 / Coherence and Cohesion / Band 7 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
Information and ideas are logically organised, and there is a clear progression throughout the response. (A few lapses may occur, but these are minor.) A range of cohesive devices including reference and substitution is used flexibly but with some inaccuracies or some over/under use. Paragraphing is generally used effectively to support overall coherence, and the sequencing of ideas within a paragraph is generally logical.

（信息与观点组织合乎逻辑，全文推进清楚；衔接手段**有变化**（含指代与替代）但偶有不准或多用少用；分段基本有效；段内顺序基本合理。）
观察点：注意 "reference and substitution"（指代与替代）—— 只会用连接词、不会用 `this shift` `such a policy` 这类指代，达不到 7。

<!-- chunk: T2.CC.6 | Task 2 / Coherence and Cohesion / Band 6 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
Information and ideas are generally arranged coherently and there is a clear overall progression. Cohesive devices are used to some good effect but cohesion within and/or between sentences may be faulty or mechanical due to misuse, overuse or omission. The use of reference and substitution may lack flexibility or clarity and result in some repetition or error. Paragraphing may not always be logical and/or the central topic may not always be clear.

（整体安排连贯、有大方向上的推进；衔接有点效果，但句内/句间衔接可能**机械或出错**（误用、滥用、漏用）；指代与替代不够灵活清楚，导致重复或错误；分段不总是合乎逻辑，段中心有时不清。）
观察点：**"mechanical"（机械）就是 6 分的官方用词**。每段开头 Firstly / Moreover / Furthermore / Lastly，正是这一条。

<!-- chunk: T2.CC.5 | Task 2 / Coherence and Cohesion / Band 5 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
Organisation is evident but is not wholly logical and there may be a lack of overall progression. Nevertheless, there is a sense of underlying coherence to the response. The relationship of ideas can be followed but the sentences are not fluently linked to each other. There may be limited/overuse of cohesive devices with some inaccuracy. The writing may be repetitive due to inadequate and/or inaccurate use of reference and substitution. Paragraphing may be inadequate or missing.

（有组织但不完全合逻辑，可能缺乏整体推进；但底层还读得出连贯感；观点关系能跟上，句子之间接得不顺；衔接手段要么太少要么过多且不准；指代不足导致重复；分段不足或没有。）

<!-- chunk: T2.CC.4 | Task 2 / Coherence and Cohesion / Band 4 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
Information and ideas are evident but not arranged coherently and there is no clear progression within the response. Relationships between ideas can be unclear and/or inadequately marked. There is some use of basic cohesive devices, which may be inaccurate or repetitive. There is inaccurate use or a lack of substitution or referencing. There may be no paragraphing and/or no clear main topic within paragraphs.

（有信息但安排不连贯，全文没有清楚推进；观点关系不清或没标出来；只用了几个基础衔接词，可能不准或重复；指代/替代缺失或错误；可能不分段，段内也没中心。）

## Lexical Resource（LR，词汇）

<!-- chunk: T2.LR.9 | Task 2 / Lexical Resource / Band 9 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
Full flexibility and precise use are widely evident. A wide range of vocabulary is used accurately and appropriately with very natural and sophisticated control of lexical features. Minor errors in spelling and word formation are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication.

（灵活与精准随处可见；词汇面宽、用得准且得体，词汇特征控制得自然老练；拼写和词形错误极少。）

<!-- chunk: T2.LR.8 | Task 2 / Lexical Resource / Band 8 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
A wide resource is fluently and flexibly used to convey precise meanings. There is skilful use of uncommon and/or idiomatic items when appropriate, despite occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation. Occasional errors in spelling and/or word formation may occur, but have minimal impact on communication.

（词汇资源宽，流畅灵活地表达精确含义；在合适的地方**用得出不常见词和地道搭配**，尽管偶有选词或搭配不准；偶有拼写/词形错误，影响极小。）
观察点："skilful use ... when appropriate" 是关键 —— 生僻词用错地方，不升反降。

<!-- chunk: T2.LR.7 | Task 2 / Lexical Resource / Band 7 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The resource is sufficient to allow some flexibility and precision. There is some ability to use less common and/or idiomatic items. An awareness of style and collocation is evident, though inappropriacies occur. There are only a few errors in spelling and/or word formation and they do not detract from overall clarity.

（词汇够用，有一定灵活度与精准度；**有一些**使用不常见词/地道表达的能力；看得出对语体和搭配有意识，但仍有不得体之处；拼写/词形错误很少，不影响整体清晰。）

<!-- chunk: T2.LR.6 | Task 2 / Lexical Resource / Band 6 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The resource is generally adequate and appropriate for the task. The meaning is generally clear in spite of a rather restricted range or a lack of precision in word choice. If the writer is a risk-taker, there will be a wider range of vocabulary used but higher degrees of inaccuracy or inappropriacy. There are some errors in spelling and/or word formation, but these do not impede communication.

（词汇总体够用、适合任务；意思大体清楚，尽管范围较窄或选词不够精准；**如果作者是冒险型，词汇面会更宽，但错误也更多**；拼写/词形有些错，不影响交流。）
观察点：这一条解释了为什么"背大词"常常不提分 —— 官方把"敢用大词但错得多"明确归在 6 分。

<!-- chunk: T2.LR.5 | Task 2 / Lexical Resource / Band 5 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The resource is limited but minimally adequate for the task. Simple vocabulary may be used accurately but the range does not permit much variation in expression. There may be frequent lapses in the appropriacy of word choice and a lack of flexibility is apparent in frequent simplifications and/or repetitions. Errors in spelling and word formation may be noticeable and may cause some difficulty for the reader.

（词汇有限但勉强够用；简单词用得对，但范围不足以变化表达；选词得体性经常出问题，反复简化或重复；拼写词形错误明显，可能造成阅读困难。）

<!-- chunk: T2.LR.4 | Task 2 / Lexical Resource / Band 4 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The resource is limited and inadequate for or unrelated to the task. Vocabulary is basic and may be used repetitively. There may be inappropriate use of lexical chunks (e.g. memorised phrases, formulaic language and/or language from the input material). Inappropriate word choice and/or errors in word formation and/or in spelling may impede meaning.

（词汇有限、不足或与任务无关；用词基础且可能重复；**可能不当使用词块（如背诵短语、公式化语言、抄材料的语言）**；选词不当、词形或拼写错误可能妨碍理解。）
观察点：这是官方把"背模板"写进扣分特征的地方。

## Grammatical Range and Accuracy（GRA，语法）

<!-- chunk: T2.GRA.9 | Task 2 / Grammatical Range and Accuracy / Band 9 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
A wide range of structures is used with full flexibility and control. Punctuation and grammar are used appropriately throughout. Minor errors are extremely rare and have minimal impact on communication.

（句式面宽、灵活且完全可控；标点与语法通篇使用恰当；小错极少。）

<!-- chunk: T2.GRA.8 | Task 2 / Grammatical Range and Accuracy / Band 8 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
A wide range of structures is flexibly and accurately used. The majority of sentences are error-free, and punctuation is well managed. Occasional, non-systematic errors and inappropriacies occur, but have minimal impact on communication.

（句式面宽、灵活准确；**多数句子零错误**；标点管理良好；偶有非系统性错误与不当之处，影响极小。）
观察点："non-systematic"（非系统性）= 错误是零星的、不是同一个毛病反复犯。同一个错犯三次，就不是 8。

<!-- chunk: T2.GRA.7 | Task 2 / Grammatical Range and Accuracy / Band 7 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
A variety of complex structures is used with some flexibility and accuracy. Grammar and punctuation are generally well controlled, and error-free sentences are frequent. A few errors in grammar may persist, but these do not impede communication.

（用得出多种复杂结构，有一定灵活度与准确度；语法标点总体控制良好，零错句**不少见**；还有一些语法错误，但不妨碍交流。）

<!-- chunk: T2.GRA.6 | Task 2 / Grammatical Range and Accuracy / Band 6 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
A mix of simple and complex sentence forms is used but flexibility is limited. Examples of more complex structures are not marked by the same level of accuracy as in simple structures. Errors in grammar and punctuation occur, but rarely impede communication.

（简单句复杂句混用，但灵活度有限；复杂结构的准确度明显不如简单结构；语法标点有错，但很少妨碍交流。）
观察点：复杂句一写就错、简单句基本没错 —— 这是 6 分的典型形态，不是 7。

<!-- chunk: T2.GRA.5 | Task 2 / Grammatical Range and Accuracy / Band 5 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
The range of structures is limited and rather repetitive. Although complex sentences are attempted, they tend to be faulty, and the greatest accuracy is achieved on simple sentences. Grammatical errors may be frequent and cause some difficulty for the reader. Punctuation may be faulty.

（句式有限且重复；复杂句一试就错，最准的是简单句；语法错误可能频繁，造成阅读困难；标点可能有问题。）

<!-- chunk: T2.GRA.4 | Task 2 / Grammatical Range and Accuracy / Band 4 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
A very limited range of structures is used. Subordinate clauses are rare and simple sentences predominate. Some structures are produced accurately but grammatical errors are frequent and may impede meaning. Punctuation is often faulty or inadequate.

（句式极其有限；从句很少，简单句为主；有些结构是对的，但语法错误频繁且妨碍理解；标点常有错。）

## Band 3–1 摘要（很少用到，但判分要有下限）

<!-- chunk: T2.LOW.3-1 | Task 2 / Band 3 到 1 摘要 | src: BC-WBD-2023 -->
**Band 3**：No part of the prompt is adequately addressed, or the prompt has been misunderstood. No relevant position can be identified.（题目没一部分答到位，或理解错题；找不到相关立场。）
**Band 2**：The content is barely related to the prompt. No position can be identified.（内容与题目几乎无关；找不到立场。）
**Band 1**：Responses of 20 words or fewer are rated at Band 1. The content is wholly unrelated to the prompt.（20 词以内；或内容与题目完全无关。）
**Band 0**：未参加、通篇非英语、或有证据证明整篇背诵。

## 中文转述的边界（重要）

上面每条的中文都是我加的一句解释，**不是官方文本**。对外输出时：

- 要"引用官方"，只能引英文原句，并把引用号一并给出（`[R:T2.TR.7]`）。
- 要"用中文讲人话"，可以翻译，但不要说"官方原话是……"。
- 中文解释与原句冲突时，**以英文原句为准**，并把冲突报给用户。
